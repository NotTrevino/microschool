Our Programs

A Path for
Every Learner

Three distinct learning tracks — asynchronous curriculum access, live general education, and live special education — all TEKS-aligned and led by a certified Texas teacher.

Track 1

K–12 Curriculum Access

Self-paced, asynchronous learning for students who need flexibility — or a supplement to their existing education.

Grades K–12 · Asynchronous

Independent Curriculum Program

Paid access to a complete, TEKS-aligned K–12 curriculum. Students work through structured, self-paced lessons on their own schedule — ideal for homeschool families, supplemental learning, or students who prefer an independent format.

What's Included

  • Full K–12 curriculum library with TEKS alignment
  • Core subjects: Math, English/ELA, Science, Social Studies
  • Optional Spanish add-on available
  • Progress tracking for parents
  • Access for additional children at reduced cost
  • Classroom licenses available for educators

Flexible by Design

Students complete lessons at their own pace with no fixed schedule. Perfect as a primary homeschool curriculum or as enrichment alongside another program.

Families can subscribe by subject or access all four core subjects together. See the Tuition page for pricing details.

Track 2

Live General Education

Structured daily instruction in a small-group online classroom — the closest thing to a real school experience, at a fraction of the size.

Grades K–12 · Live Online · Mon–Thu

General Education Live Program

Three hours of live, teacher-led instruction every weekday (Monday through Thursday) in core subjects. Maximum eight students per class ensures every child is seen, heard, and supported — not just present.

What's Included

  • 3 hours of live instruction daily (15 hours/week)
  • Core subjects: Math, English, Science, Social Studies
  • Maximum 8 students per class
  • TEKS-aligned curriculum and assessments
  • Weekly parent progress updates
  • Quarterly parent-teacher conferences
  • Interactive, real-time instruction (not recorded videos)

Track 3

Special Education Program

Intensive, individualized live instruction for students who need more dedicated support, smaller groups, and a teacher who truly understands their learning profile.

Grades K–12 · Live Online · 4 Days/Week

Special Education Live Program

Four days of live instruction per week with two hours of dedicated daily support — designed for students with IEPs, learning differences, or those who need a more structured, supported approach to daily learning.

What's Included

  • 2 hours of live instruction daily, 4 days/week (8 hrs/week)
  • Maximum 8 students per class
  • Individualized instructional approach
  • Accommodations built into daily instruction
  • TEKS-aligned curriculum at appropriate level
  • Weekly communication with parents
  • Progress documentation and reporting

Designed for Students Who Need More

Our special education program is structured for students who benefit from consistent routines, smaller groups, patient pacing, and a teacher who adapts instruction to the individual — not the class average.

During your discovery call, we'll discuss your child's specific needs and confirm whether this program is the right fit.
